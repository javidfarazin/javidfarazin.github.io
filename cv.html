About

I am a physicist working on optical tweezers and the software that runs them. I did my MSc at IASBS in Zanjan, and before that a BSc in physics at the University of Mohaghegh Ardabili. I like work that sits between the optical table and the code: aligning a setup in the morning and training a model in the afternoon.

Most of my published papers come from my BSc years in a materials lab, where I learned the experimental side of physics — sample preparation, spectroscopy, and electrical measurements. The optical tweezers work started later, during the MSc, and it is what I want to keep doing.

I speak Azerbaijani, Persian and Turkish, and I work in English.
