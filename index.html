About me

Physicist exploring laser micromanipulation, intelligent microscopy, and machine learning — at the boundary where optics meets AI.

I am Javid Farazin, an optical physicist with a Master of Science in Optics and Photonics from the Institute for Advanced Studies in Basic Sciences (IASBS), Zanjan, Iran. My graduate research, conducted within the Optical Micromanipulation Group under the supervision of Dr. Ehsan Ahadi-Akhlaghi and Dr. Faegheh Hajizadeh, centred on AI-Powered Automation of Optical Tweezers: integrating reinforcement learning and deep neural networks with real-time microscope image streams to enable autonomous control of laser-trapped microspheres.

I built and calibrated a complete optical tweezers setup, then developed both a reinforcement learning controller for collision-aware velocity control and a deep learning object detection pipeline for particle tracking within the trap — work I presented at the 4th International Conference on Light and Light-Based Technologies (ICLLT 2024) in Ankara. A journal article expanding this research — Image-Guided Control and Monitoring of Optical Tweezers with Artificial Neural Network — is currently in preparation (2026).

I currently serve as a Research and Development Specialist at Phylabs Knowledge-Based Company, IASBS Science and Technology Park, where I develop machine learning and deep learning systems for biomedical classification in applied photonics, including models that distinguish biological samples from visually similar substances using image and spectral data.

My broader interests span biophotonics, super-resolution microscopy, nonlinear and quantum optics, and physics-informed machine learning. My earlier work at the University of Mohaghegh Ardabili on Schottky diode fabrication, nanomaterial synthesis, and optical characterisation gives me a wide experimental foundation. I have co-authored 12 peer-reviewed journal articles in Springer and Elsevier journals and 8 conference papers.

You can find my full list of publications and CV in the sections below. Feel free to contact me for inquiries or collaborations.
