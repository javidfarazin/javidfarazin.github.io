Research

I work on optical tweezers and on the software that runs them. Most of my work sits between the optical table and the code: building the setup, recording what happens, and teaching a program to control it.

01

Optical tweezers and automated particle manipulation

Optical tweezers hold a small particle with light. A laser beam is focused to a tight spot, and the gradient of the light field pulls the particle toward that spot. Under a microscope you can watch the trapped particle and move it through the sample.

Doing this by hand is slow. You watch the screen, move the stage, and correct the path yourself. My MSc project at IASBS started from a simple question: can a program do this on its own?

The answer turned out to be yes. I built a method that guides a trapped bead to a chosen point in the sample, and tested it on the setup in our lab. The work became my MSc thesis, supervised by Dr. Ehsan Ahadi-Akhlaghi and Dr. Faegheh Hajizadeh. An early part of it won first prize for best poster at ICLLT 2024 in Ankara.

The method itself is not published yet, so I am keeping the details off this page until the paper is out. If you would like to talk about it, write to me.

02

Machine learning for photonics instruments

Between 2024 and 2026 I worked at the Science and Technology Park at IASBS, where I built machine learning and deep learning tools for photonics instruments.

Most of that work is not public, so I keep the description short here. If you want to know more about the methods, write to me.

Before that, from 2022 to 2024, I worked part time at Aqua Solve Zanjan, a company founded by Prof. Yousef Sobouti. The project looked at how to make fresh water for rural homes from sea and brackish water, using solar and wind energy.

03

Earlier work: nanomaterials and semiconductor interfaces

During my BSc I worked as a research assistant at the Faculty of Advanced Technologies, University of Mohaghegh Ardabili, with Prof. Yashar Azizian-Kalandaragh. We prepared nanostructures and polymer nanocomposites, and studied how a thin interfacial layer changes the electrical behavior of Al/p-Si Schottky diodes.

Those years taught me the experimental side of physics: sample preparation, FTIR and optical spectroscopy, wettability measurements, and current-voltage and impedance measurements. Most of my journal papers come from this period, which is why the publication list looks different from the projects above.
